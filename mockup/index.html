One thing to fix in the sprite. Flip to the world view and watch the face. The eyes and smile are dark, and dark means absent on an additive display — over a bright sky the expression drops out and only the silhouette survives. A 1–2px bright ring around each eye and the smile, plus a brighter rim on the body edge, keeps the character readable against sky, pavement and a white wall alike.
